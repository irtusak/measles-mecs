# Data sources

Every number in this dashboard comes from one of the sources below, or is computed from them by code in
`scripts/` and `R/`. Modelled scenario values are generated by `R/model.R` and are always labelled as modelled.

Run `Rscript scripts/fetch_data.R` to refresh. It records the URL, byte size, SHA-256 and download time of every
file in `data/raw/manifest.json`.

---

## 1. Measles surveillance — Public Health Agency of Canada

**Measles and Rubella Weekly Monitoring Report**
Landing page: https://health-infobase.canada.ca/measles-rubella/
Licence: Open Government Licence – Canada. Required attribution, used throughout this project:
*Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.*

These are the machine-readable CSVs that drive PHAC's own published figures. They are not linked from the
page as downloads; the file list was read from the page's `data-loader.js`. Base path:
`https://health-infobase.canada.ca/src/data/measles-rubella/`

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `global_variables.csv` | Headline counts, report year and week, active cases, multi-jurisdictional outbreak totals |
| `figure2A-EpiCurvePT.csv` | Weekly confirmed cases by province/territory, **current reporting year only** |
| `figure3-epi-curve-yearly.csv` | Annual confirmed cases, 1998 onward; probable cases for 2025–2026 |
| `geographic_distribution.csv` | Cases by province/territory, year to date, with last week of rash onset |
| `demographics.csv` | Age group, sex, exposure source, vaccination status, genotype, hospitalisations, deaths |
| `outbreaks.csv` | Outbreak-level detail by jurisdiction, including last rash onset year and week |
| `Figure3-ActiveMap.csv` | Active cases by health region |
| `updateDate.csv` | PHAC's own "data as of" timestamp |

**Known limitations, carried into the app:**

- The weekly epidemic curve covers the **current reporting year only**. PHAC does not publish a weekly file
  for 2025, so the multi-year view uses the annual series. A 2025 weekly curve is not reconstructed.
- Epidemiological weeks after the current reporting week are blank in the weekly file. PHAC's own "Canada"
  column carries a literal `0` for those weeks; the app treats them as not yet reported.
- Confirmed and probable cases are reported separately and are never silently summed.
- `Figure3-ActiveMap.csv` has blank cells for health regions with no active cases.
- Some counts in `outbreaks.csv` are blank for individual jurisdictions (for example Saskatchewan's
  confirmed/probable split), and small percentages in `demographics.csv` appear as `<1`.

## 2. Immunization coverage — Statistics Canada / PHAC

**Table 13-10-0870-01 — Vaccine coverage estimates for recommended vaccines in children and pregnant women**
Source of the estimates: PHAC **childhood National Immunization Coverage Survey (cNICS)**
Table: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1310087001
CSV: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/tbl/csv/13100870-eng.zip
Open data record: https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/39b3c9a9-608f-44e0-9d25-8f98616323dd
Licence: Statistics Canada Open Licence. Because the estimates are reshaped and re-plotted here, this is a
"value-added product" under that licence, and the required notice is used throughout:
*Adapted from Statistics Canada, table 13-10-0870-01, 2021. This does not constitute an endorsement by
Statistics Canada of this product.*

Provides measles coverage by province/territory for 2-, 7- and 17-year-olds, with 95% confidence intervals.
The survey is biennial: 2011, 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, **2021 (latest machine-readable cycle)**.

**Quality flags** are carried through to the app and decide what may be displayed:

| Flag | Meaning | How the app treats it |
|---|---|---|
| *(blank)* | Usable estimate | Shown normally |
| `E` | Use with caution | Shown with a caution marker and its confidence interval |
| `F` | Too unreliable to be published | Gap — never shown as a number |
| `x` | Suppressed to meet confidentiality requirements | Gap — labelled "suppressed" |
| `..` | Not available for this reference period | Gap — labelled "not surveyed" |

Of 252 province × age-group × cycle combinations for measles, **80 carry a usable estimate** and 172 are
suppressed or not available. Not every jurisdiction is sampled in every cycle, which is why provincial
series have gaps between cycles.

**Note on the project brief.** The brief describes 2021 coverage for 2-year-olds as ranging "77% to 92%".
The table's actual 2021 measles figures for 2-year-olds run from **34.6% (Nunavut, flagged `E`)** to
**98.0% (Prince Edward Island)**, with Canada at **91.6%**. The dashboard uses the table.

## 3. Transmission parameters — published literature

Not a downloaded dataset. Values are cited inline in `R/model.R` and justified in `docs/METHODS.md`.
They are taken from the published literature and are **not** tuned to fit Canadian case counts.
